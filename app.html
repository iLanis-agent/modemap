Axial modes

About this

Natural frequencies of an empty rectangular room with rigid walls: f = (c/2) × sqrt((p/L)² + (q/W)² + (r/H)²). Real rooms with furniture, soft walls and openings shift these and damp them. Close axial pairs (within 5%) are flagged as a rough heuristic. The Schroeder frequency is 2000 × sqrt(RT60 / volume in m³). Speed of sound uses 331.3 + 0.606 × °C. Treat this as a planning map, not a measurement. Sources: Schroeder frequency, Vibrationdata, rectangular room modes, Rindel, room proportions. About
